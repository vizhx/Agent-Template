### Switzerland: Alpine Beauty & Precision

Switzerland is a landlocked federal republic in Europe, known for its stunning landscapes and 26 cantons.

## Geography & Nature
Dominated by the **Swiss Alps**, it features iconic peaks like the Matterhorn and beautiful lakes such as Lake Geneva.

## Culture & Languages
A multilingual nation, Switzerland recognizes four official languages: German, French, Italian, and Romansh.

## Economy
The Swiss economy is globally renowned for precision watchmaking, international banking, and world-class chocolate and cheese.

## Neutrality
Maintaining armed neutrality since 1815, Switzerland hosts many international organizations, including the Red Cross and United Nations agencies.
